Social Media Image Sizes Guide (Instagram, Facebook, X, YouTube & More)

By the Teknawy team · November 13, 2026 · 6 min read

Every social network crops and compresses images in its own way. Uploading at the recommended size keeps your photos sharp and stops important details from being cut off. Below are commonly recommended sizes for the most popular platforms.

Platforms change their layouts from time to time. These are widely used recommendations; check each platform's help centre if something looks off.

TikTok and Pinterest

  • TikTok video cover and photo posts: 1080 × 1920 (9:16).
  • Pinterest standard pin: 1000 × 1500 (2:3).

Tips for sharp social media images

  • Upload at the recommended size — not much larger. Platforms recompress big uploads more aggressively.
  • Keep text and faces away from the edges, where crops happen.
  • Use JPEG for photos and PNG for graphics with text; both are accepted everywhere.
