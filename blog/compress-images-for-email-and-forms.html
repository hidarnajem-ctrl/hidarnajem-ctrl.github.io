How to Shrink Photos for Email and Online Forms

By the Teknawy team · November 13, 2026 · 5 min read

“File too large.” It is one of the most frustrating messages when you are applying for a job, filling in a government form or emailing photos to family. Here is how to make any photo small enough in under a minute, on a phone or a computer.

Know the limit

First, find the exact requirement. Forms usually state something like “JPG only, max 100 KB” or “maximum 2 MB per file”. Some also specify dimensions, for example “600 × 600 pixels”. Note both the size limit and any dimensions.

For email, most providers allow attachments of roughly 20–25 MB in total, but big attachments are slow to send and download. Keeping each photo under 1 MB is a comfortable target.

Method 1: Set a target size (easiest)

  1. Open the Compress to 100KB tool.
  2. Enter the required maximum in the Target file size field — for example 100, 50 or 200.
  3. Add your photo. The tool automatically finds the highest quality that fits under the limit.
  4. Download the result and upload it to the form.

Method 2: Resize and compress manually

  1. Open the image compressor and add your photos.
  2. Choose a maximum width: 1280px is plenty for email; use the form's required size if it has one.
  3. Set quality to around 75% and JPEG as the output format.
  4. Check the new size shown next to each file. If it is still too large, lower the width or quality a little.

Tips for official documents

  • Use JPEG. It is accepted almost everywhere; WEBP is not always accepted by official portals.
  • Crop first. Remove empty background around a passport photo or signature before compressing — fewer pixels means a smaller file at better quality.
  • Good lighting helps. Photos with less noise compress much better.
  • Privacy matters. ID photos and documents contain personal data. Teknawy processes them on your own device, so they are never uploaded to a third-party server.

On a phone

Teknawy works in mobile browsers too. Open teknawy.click, tap “Choose images”, select the photo from your gallery, set the target size and tap Download. The compressed file is saved to your downloads folder, ready to attach.
