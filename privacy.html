Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Teknawy ("we", "us") at https://teknawy.click handles information when you use our website.

1. Your images

All image compression, resizing and conversion is performed entirely on your device, inside your web browser. Your images are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party, and we cannot see, store or access them. When you close or refresh the page, the images are removed from your browser's memory.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, address or phone number. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message, which we use only to reply to you.

Your language choice may be saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. This stays on your device.

To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

4. Log data

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and operation of the service.

5. Children's privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
