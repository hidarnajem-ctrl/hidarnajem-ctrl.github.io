Why convert PNG to WEBP?

PNG is a lossless format. It is perfect for logos, icons and screenshots because it keeps every pixel exactly as it was, but that precision comes at a price: PNG files are often several times larger than they need to be for the web. WEBP is a modern format developed by Google that supports both lossy and lossless compression and transparency (alpha channel). In practice, converting a PNG to WEBP usually reduces the file size by 50–80% while the image looks the same to the eye.

Smaller images mean faster pages, lower hosting bandwidth and a better experience for visitors on mobile data. Page speed is also part of Google's page experience signals, so lighter images can help your site in search results.

  3. Download — save each WEBP image individually or download all of them as a single ZIP file.

Does WEBP keep transparency?

Yes. Unlike JPEG, WEBP supports a full alpha channel, so transparent backgrounds in your PNG logos, stickers and icons are preserved after conversion.

Is WEBP supported everywhere?

All modern browsers — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari (since version 14) and Opera — display WEBP images, as do WordPress and most website builders. Some older desktop software and a few upload forms still only accept JPG or PNG; in that case use our format converter to export JPEG instead, or keep a PNG copy.

Tips for the best result

  • For screenshots containing small text, use a higher quality (85–95%) to keep the text crisp.
  • For photos saved as PNG by mistake, a quality of 70–80% gives huge savings.
  • If the image is larger than it will ever be displayed, set a maximum width (for example 1280px) — resizing often saves more than compression alone.

Frequently asked questions

Will converting PNG to WEBP reduce quality?

At quality 80% or above the difference is practically invisible for most images. If you need pixel-perfect output, set the quality to 100%.

Are my PNG files uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion uses your browser's built-in image engine, so the files never leave your device.

Can I convert many PNG files at once?

Yes. Add as many files as you like and download them all together as a ZIP archive.
