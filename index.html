Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Teknawy compresses and resizes images entirely inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your files never leave your device.

Which image formats are supported?

You can add JPG/JPEG, PNG and WEBP images and export them as JPEG, PNG or WEBP.

What quality setting should I use?

A quality of 70–80% usually reduces file size by 50–80% with no visible difference. For the smallest files, choose WEBP output.

Is there a limit on file size or number of images?

There are no limits or paywalls. Because processing happens on your device, the only limit is your device's memory.

Why did my PNG not get smaller?

PNG is a lossless format, so the quality slider does not apply to it. Convert to WEBP or JPEG, or reduce the dimensions, to make PNG files much smaller.
